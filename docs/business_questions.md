# Business Questions

## 1. Revenue Performance
- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many orders were placed?
- What is the average order value?
- How does revenue change over time?

## 2. Customer Behavior
- How do new and returning customers contribute to revenue?
- How much revenue does each customer category generate per customer?
- How frequently do customers make repeat purchases?

## 3. Customer Retention
- How does retention change across customer cohorts?
- What percentage of customers return in subsequent months?
- Which customer cohorts demonstrate stronger retention?

## 4. Customer Segmentation
- Which customers have the highest recency, frequency, and monetary values?
- Which customers may need re-engagement?
- How can RFM segmentation support customer retention strategies?
